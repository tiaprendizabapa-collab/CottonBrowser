CottonBrowser Android filter data

easylist_domains.txt and easylist_allow.tsv are modified subsets of EasyList,
extracted offline from the uBlock Origin Lite 2026.914.1325 snapshot included in
this repository. The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/) are the source of
the filter material. uBlock Origin Lite is maintained by Raymond Hill and its
contributors (https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock).

Changes: only unconditional domain-block rules are extracted; third-party,
resource-type, URL-path and other conditional block rules are not broadened.
All allow rules from that snapshot retain URL/request/initiator constraints.
Their party/resource-type constraints are relaxed to avoid false blocking in
WebView, which does not provide those types. This subset is not the uBlock
Origin Lite extension or a complete implementation of EasyList.

blocklist.txt and the page/YouTube protection scripts are CottonBrowser's
separate local protection rules. All data is bundled; no browsing URLs are
sent to a filtering service and no remote filtering lookup is performed.
